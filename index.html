Three Decades of Vision

In 1996, Mark Hachem opened his first contemporary art gallery in the heart of Paris, in the Marais. Eleven years later, he inaugurated a second space on Madison Avenue in New York. Following the successful opening of a Beirut gallery in 2010, Hachem further expanded his engagement with artistic movements across the Middle Eastern and Asian markets.

Over the past two decades, the gallery has showcased renowned artists, initially distinguishing itself among Parisian galleries through its focus on Kinetic art. Mark Hachem continues to exhibit leading figures of the Op Art movement while participating in major contemporary art fairs worldwide.

With more than 300 exhibitions to its credit, Mark Hachem Gallery has established an international presence through its exhibitions and participation in art fairs. Mark Hachem is also active as an adviser to public institutions and private collectors.

Retrospective
7 March — 12 July 2026 Tourcoing

Hamed Abdalla — Signes d’Égypte

A major retrospective spanning five decades

Institut du monde arabe–Tourcoing · Curated by Nada Majdoub

The Institut du monde arabe–Tourcoing presents a major retrospective dedicated to Hamed Abdalla (1917–1985), one of the defining figures of modern Egyptian art. Bringing together paintings, drawings, lithographs, and archival materials preserved by the artist’s family, the exhibition retraces the successive phases of his life and work — from the “Signs of Egypt” series to the anthropomorphic Arabic letterforms of his Paris years.

Sara Shamma at the Venice Biennale 2026

The Tower Tomb of Palmyra — Syrian Pavilion

61st International Art Exhibition — La Biennale di Venezia · Curated by Yuko Hasegawa

Sara Shamma has been selected for the 61st Venice Biennale. Commissioned by the Syrian Ministry of Culture and curated by Yuko Hasegawa — director of the 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art in Kanazawa — the multi-sensory pavilion “The Tower Tomb of Palmyra” will occupy the open-air courtyard of the Università IUAV di Venezia, focusing on the ancient city once ruled by the warrior queen Zenobia.
